Frequently Asked Questions

Browse answers to the questions most commonly asked by students and parents.

Which syllabus do you teach?

We provide coaching exclusively for CBSE Grade 10 and Grade 12 students following the latest CBSE curriculum.

Which subjects are available?

We offer Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry coaching with experienced subject experts.

Are study materials provided?

Yes. Students receive worksheets, handwritten notes, revision guides, formula books, question banks, and previous year papers.

How are student assessments conducted?

Students participate in daily practice tests, weekly assessments, monthly examinations, chapter tests, and board model examinations.

Do you conduct revision classes?

Yes. Special revision sessions, doubt clarification programs, and board examination preparation classes are conducted throughout the academic year.

Are parents informed about student progress?

Yes. Regular parent meetings and academic performance reports help families monitor student progress effectively.

Can students join in the middle of the academic year?

Admission depends on seat availability and academic evaluation. Contact the institute for eligibility and admission guidance.

How can I enroll?

Visit our institute, call us, send a WhatsApp message, or submit the online admission enquiry form.
